## Software Development Lifecycle

#### How do I interpret user needs and implement them into a program? How does creating “user stories” help with this? 

Through the lens of scrum, user stories are a high-level and human way of attaining product specifications. The user stories can be translated into features or tasks that are implemented by the developers. User stories act as middlemen between customers and developers and help give an intuitive sense of product features and development. 

#### How do I approach developing programs? What agile processes do I hope to incorporate into my future development work?

As a hobby engineer, I have approached developing programs in much the same way agile does within a group. I hope to one day be a part of a team for future development work. It would help to be involved in an agile team to acquire real world experience and become acquainted with the language. 

#### What does it mean to be a good team member in software development? 

I would think that working in a tech company environment would be comparable with any other kind of industrial or service environment. I believe as work modernizes it helps to be lenient toward your coworkers. One should work in a healthy manner, allow others to work in a healthy manner, and be a part of the team.
